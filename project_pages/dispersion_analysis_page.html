Dispersion Analysis Modeling Research

    I am currently working with Dr. Pal with dispersion analysis research where we are wanting to analyze wave propagation in lattices with COMSOL Multiphysics modeling. The first roadblock I had to address was creating a program that could analyze lattices sketched out and make extruded parts on SolidWorks (see above).
    So far, this program accounts for lines, arcs, and circle commands using SolidWorks API calls. The next steps will be importing these models onto COMSOL Multypyshics to study how the behave in simulations. We are already running experiments looking at the stiffnesses for different lattices with dynamic excitations set with a prescribed displacement and frequency at a central point.
