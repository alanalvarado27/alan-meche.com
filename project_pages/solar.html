Texas A&M Solar Car Racing - Suspension Engineer

    I am on the Suspension Subteam for the '26 - '27 year. Over the summer, we determined the appropriate track width of the car, allowing us to set our suspension points using an in-house calculator to accomodate forces on a double-wishbone suspension system.
    I am currently setting bonding geometry for carbon-fiber tubing epoxied with aluminum inserts for the A-Arms in our suspension system. Using carbon-fiber tubing would allow use to heavily decrease the weight due to the suspension system, since last year a solid steel material was used.
    I am setting calculations for the axial displacement and maximum shear stresses of parts in the bonding section (aluminum insert, epoxy, and carbon-fiber tubing) at expected maximum forces to determine the optimal geometry for these components and their distance. I am also determining tensile, compression, and testing parameters (strain rate) for tests on our Instron machines to find bonding properties for the specific materials we order.
